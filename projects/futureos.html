Exhibit 04 · Live product, thin evidence

FutureOS

A decision operating system that keeps the contract that existed before anyone knew the answer: the forecast, the update, and the outcome, scored separately from luck. The public site is online. It does not yet claim that use improves judgment. Product: futureos.space. Repository: DresdenGman/futureos.

The thinnest evidence of the four exhibits. The public impact ledger, read 2026-11-17, records 2 instrument starts and 2 completions, observed 2026-08-31 to 2026-09-07. Nothing on this page should be read as an active user base — the ledger itself states that a start is not a user.

The problem

Decisions should remember why they changed

Most software remembers what happened. FutureOS keeps what someone believed before the outcome was known, why they acted, and how the evidence updated — then scores the quality of the process separately from the luck of the result, with rules such as Brier scoring.

What I built

The loop and the methods

  • The loop: Frame → forecast → update → resolve.
  • Methods, offered as transparent heuristics rather than general scientific estimators: expected utility, Bayesian revision, Brier scoring, option value.
  • Open instruments need no account: the Decision Quality Score and the Probability Calibration Test. Authenticated workspaces are sign-in gated. Stack: React 19, TypeScript, Vinext, D1.

Evidence

What can be checked

  • The product site is live: futureos.space. That is a live site — not a claim of an active user base.
  • Public impact ledger (futureos.space/impact, read 2026-11-17; observation window 2026-08-31 to 2026-09-07): 2 participant-days; 2 instrument starts; 2 completions. By instrument — Decision Quality Score: 2 starts, 2 completions, 1 share action. Probability Calibration Test: 0 starts, 0 completions, 0 shares. The page itself states that a start is not called a user, and a completion is not called an outcome.
  • Research protocol and privacy note are published: futureos.space/research and futureos.space/privacy — hypotheses, metrics and failure conditions are written down there.
  • Repository: github.com/DresdenGman/futureos.

Timeline

Public record only

  • 2026-06-16 — public repository created (GitHub record).
  • Online as of 2026-11-17 — the public site, the two open instruments, and the public impact ledger. No growth line is published: the ledger above is the whole of it.

Limits and next steps

What it cannot do yet

  • The README states it plainly: there is no claim yet that using FutureOS improves judgment. Longitudinal evidence would need consented participants, repeated resolved decisions, and reporting of side effects.
  • The public impact ledger is tiny, and test coverage is thin. Both are stated here rather than rounded up.
  • The next step is people running real decisions end to end, at a meaningful scale, with the outcomes recorded — not stronger product language.
