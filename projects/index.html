Decision OS · live product

FutureOS

Decisions should remember why they changed. FutureOS turns a decision into a contract before anyone acts — the question, a probability, a success rule, a deadline — then tracks how the belief updates until it resolves, so process quality can be separated from the luck of the final outcome. Loop: frame, forecast, update, resolve; scored with Bayesian revision and Brier scores.

Benchmark · energy

PJM Cold-Calibration Benchmark

Can calibration learned from past cold spells be transferred to the next one? During the 2014 polar vortex, day-ahead QR-GBT 90% coverage was 12.5%, compared with 65.7% over the year.
