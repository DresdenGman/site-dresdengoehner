Decision OS · live product

FutureOS

Decisions should remember why they changed. FutureOS turns a decision into a contract before anyone acts — the question, a probability, a success rule, a deadline — then tracks how the belief updates until it resolves, so process quality can be separated from the luck of the final outcome. Loop: frame, forecast, update, resolve; scored with Bayesian revision and Brier scores.

Benchmark · energy

PJM Cold-Calibration Benchmark

Can calibration learned from past cold spells be transferred to the next one? During the 2014 polar vortex, day-ahead QR-GBT 90% coverage fell to 12.5% from 65.7% over the year; 0 of 18 repair cells passed.

Repository · DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24010850 · Written up on the GERT page

Research report · quantitative finance

The Backtest That Lied

A self-falsification report: an A-share ranking signal collapsed from IC 0.94 to 0.10 after leakage was removed, and did not survive costs or liquid universes. The OHLCV-only branch was terminated.

Stated plainly: the original training and the RQAlpha data are not in the repository, so the report’s “200+ fits” cannot be reproduced from the repository. All 15 commits are dated 2026-07-26.
